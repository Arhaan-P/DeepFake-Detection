# Gemini image-generation prompts — conceptual figures (Category A)

These four figures are conceptual/illustrative and are **not** generated by the
plotting scripts in `scripts/generate_figures/`. Generate each one with Gemini
using the prompt below, then save it to the **exact filename given in the
heading**. `deepfake_paper.tex` already references those paths with final captions
written, and wraps each `\includegraphics` in an `\IfFileExists` guard, so the
document compiles with labelled placeholder boxes until you drop the PNGs in.

**Global constraints — repeat these in every prompt if the model drifts:**

- Clean academic/technical diagram style. Flat vector look, no 3-D bevels, no
  drop shadows, no gradients, no glow, no photorealism, no decorative
  illustration.
- White background. No border frame around the whole image.
- Sans-serif labels throughout (Inter, Helvetica or Arial), consistently sized,
  horizontal — never rotated or set on a curve.
- Palette, used consistently across all four figures so they read as one set:
  - deep blue `#1B6CA8` — the authentic/enrolled path, primary boxes
  - light blue `#C8D8E4` — secondary fills, passive/auxiliary blocks
  - red `#C0504D` — the deepfake/attack path, mismatch outcomes
  - amber `#E08214` — decision points, thresholds, highlights
  - grey `#4D4D4D` — text, arrows, rules; `#D9D9D9` — hairlines
- Every arrow must have a single clear head and must not cross another arrow.
- **Spell every label exactly as written.** Do not paraphrase, pluralise, or
  "improve" the wording. No invented labels, no lorem-ipsum, no extra icons.
- 300 DPI equivalent, wide aspect unless stated otherwise.

If Gemini garbles the text (common with dense diagrams), generate the layout
and re-letter it in Inkscape/Figma rather than accepting misspelled labels — a
misspelled block label is worse than a placeholder box.

## 4. `figures/fig4_architecture.png`

**Figure role in the paper:** Fig. 4, the network diagram in Section III-E/F.

**This is the figure that must be drawn accurately.** It has to reflect what the
code in `models/full_pipeline.py` actually does, not the older README diagram.
Two facts the diagram must convey, or it is wrong:

1. The **verification logits come from the difference-based CNN applied to the
   raw 78-dim paired features** — not from the encoder or the temporal model.
2. The **GaitEncoder → DualPathTemporalModel branch is an auxiliary embedding
   path.** It is present in the checkpoint and produces the embedding used for
   diagnostics and enrolment analysis, but it does not feed the classifier and
   receives no gradient from the cross-entropy objective.

Dimensions below are from `outputs/model_config.json` (`encoder_output_dim`
= 128, `lstm_hidden` = 64, `transformer_d_model` = 128, 4 heads, 2 layers,
`verification_hidden` = 64) — do not substitute larger values from older docs.

**Aspect ratio:** wide, approximately 16:9 (set across both columns).

**Prompt:**

> A clean, flat neural-network architecture diagram, academic paper style,
> white background, sans-serif labels, no 3-D blocks, no shadows, no gradients.
> Boxes are rounded rectangles with a title line in bold and a smaller grey
> tensor-shape line beneath.
>
> Two inputs at the far left, stacked vertically, each a light blue `#C8D8E4`
> box:
> - upper: "Video gait features", sub-line "(B, 60, 78)"
> - lower: "Claimed identity signature", sub-line "(B, 60, 78)"
>
> The diagram then splits into two clearly separated horizontal lanes. Draw a
> thin dashed grey horizontal rule between the lanes and label each lane at its
> far left with small vertical-stack heading text.
>
> LOWER LANE — heading "Verification path (trained)". This lane is drawn in
> deep blue `#1B6CA8` with solid 2px arrows, and is visually dominant.
> Both inputs feed into it. Sequence:
> 1. A box labelled "Pairwise comparison", sub-line
>    "[ d ‖ |d| ‖ v ⊙ c ] → (B, 60, 234)". Below the box, small grey text:
>    "d = v − c".
> 2. Arrow to a box labelled "Temporal CNN", with three stacked sub-lines:
>    "Conv1D 234→64, k=7", "Conv1D 64→64, k=5", "Conv1D 64→32, k=3". Add a
>    small grey note under it: "BatchNorm + ReLU + Dropout".
> 3. Arrow to a small box "Global average pool", sub-line "(B, 32)".
> 4. Arrow to a small box "MLP 32→32→2".
> 5. Arrow to a final box with an amber `#E08214` outline and white fill,
>    labelled "Softmax", sub-line "P(authentic)".
>
> UPPER LANE — heading "Auxiliary embedding path". Draw this lane entirely in
> light blue `#C8D8E4` fills with grey `#4D4D4D` labels and **dashed** 1.5px
> arrows, visibly secondary to the lower lane. Only the "Video gait features"
> input feeds it. Sequence:
> 1. "GaitEncoder — 1D CNN", sub-line "78 → 64 → 128, 2 residual blocks",
>    second sub-line "(B, 60, 128)".
> 2. Arrow to a container box labelled "DualPathTemporalModel" that visually
>    encloses two smaller side-by-side boxes: "BiLSTM" with sub-line
>    "h=64, 1 layer, bidirectional → 128", and "Transformer Encoder" with
>    sub-line "d=128, 4 heads, 2 layers". Beneath the two, inside the same
>    container, a narrow box "Fusion MLP → 128".
> 3. Arrow to a box "Gait embedding", sub-line "(B, 128)".
> 4. Arrow to a final box drawn with a **dashed** grey outline and white fill,
>    labelled "Diagnostics / enrolment analysis".
>
> Critically: draw **no arrow of any kind** from the upper lane into the lower
> lane. The two lanes must never join. This separation is the point of the
> figure.
>
> At the far right, spanning the height of both lanes, place a tall narrow

> containing three short left-aligned lines:
> "133,058 trainable parameters on the verification path",
> "715,556 parameters off it",
> "cross-entropy is applied to the softmax output only".
>
> No title, no legend, no other text.

   alongside the seven script-generated plates, not in `diagrams/`).

   The `\IfFileExists` guards resolve to the real images automatically; nothing
   in `deepfake_paper.tex` needs editing.

   submission — it is the figure a reviewer is most likely to check against the
   released code.
